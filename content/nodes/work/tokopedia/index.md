I joined  **Tokopedia’s Fintech team as a Data Analyst** , working in an end-to-end data role that covered data engineering, business and product analytics, marketing analytics, data visualization, and data science.

Initially assigned as adata analyst for  **Merchant Lending** , a credit product for Tokopedia merchants. I partnered closely with product and business teams to identify growth opportunities and support product strategy, contributing to the product growing approximately **5x** during my time on the team.

Following that experience, I was given the opportunity to become the  **go-to Data Analyst for Tokopedia’s BNPL product**, owning analytics across the product lifecycle; from acquisition and activation to engagement, monetization, pricing, credit limits, and promotions. I worked closely with Business and Product leadership to translate complex customer and transaction data into product strategy and growth initiatives, helping scale the business to ~USD 200M **in two years** .

Overall, Tokopedia was where I developed from a data analyst into an  **end-to-end data and strategic business partner** —combining technical execution, product thinking, and commercial problem-solving to help build and scale fintech products.

My term in Tokopedia ends when Bytedance acquired Tokopedia, while I'm survived the multiple rounds of layoffs, I decided that I cannot stay after my whole team were laid off.
