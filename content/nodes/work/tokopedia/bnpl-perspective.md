## Context

Cumulated learnings from years of owning BNPL fintech analytics distilled into the factors that consistently determine whether a Buy-Now-Pay-Later product actually gets adopted.

## The five factors

**1. Eligible user base.** A BNPL product is only as big as the pool of users who are both
eligible and willing to apply, and that pool is usually much smaller than the total user base.
Widening eligibility without also improving targeting rarely moves the needle much on its own.

**2. Pricing.** BNPL users are very sensitive to interest rates. Small changes in
monthly interest translate almost linearly into changes in transaction volume.

**3. Credit limits.** The right limit depends entirely on the category. A limit that's generous
for daily essentials can be far too low for electronics or big-ticket purchases, and limit
increases reliably drive both adoption and spend.

**4. Promotions.** Promotions are a powerful lever. Locking a promotion
to a longer repayment tenure shifts real behavior, while short-term discounting mostly shifts
payment method rather than creating new demand.

**5. Acceptance beyond one use case.** A BNPL product that only works in one place struggles to
build loyalty. The products that win are the ones usable cross-platform, so a user never
needs a second BNPL provider.
