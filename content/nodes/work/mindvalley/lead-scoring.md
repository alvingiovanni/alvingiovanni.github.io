## Problem

The lead database was large but underused. A significant share of leads had gone cold, while the communication sent to them remained generic.

Leads received roughly the same volume of messaging whether they were marketing qualified (MQL) or sales qualified (SQL). As a result, emails often did not reflect each lead’s readiness, and there was no **non-sales journey for warming cold leads** before introducing sales communication.

## Solution

Built a **lead scoring model** based on each lead’s actions across marketing activities and interactions with the product.

The model assigned every lead a score and grouped them into **communication tiers**. Marketing used these tiers to vary communication by lead readiness, nurture colder leads with non-sales content, and help leads progress to higher scores.

## Impact

- Increased advertising conversion rate by 190%
- Increased lifecycle conversion by 14%
- Increased sales email conversion by 26%
