## Problem

Direct attribution had become less reliable as **data privacy changes and advertising-platform restrictions** reduced visibility across customer journeys.

This made it harder to determine which channels generated incremental revenue, how media spend related to revenue, and how quickly that investment paid back.

## Solution

Built and validated a **media mix model** that measured the relationship between media spend and revenue across channels.

The model assessed **incrementality and advertising payback**, giving the business a consistent basis for comparing channel performance, identifying diminishing returns, and allocating media budgets more efficiently.
