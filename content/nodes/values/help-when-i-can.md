---
title: Help When I Can
order: 1
---

When I see someone struggling, I try not to assume what they need. I ask directly whether they are having difficulty and whether they would like a hand. **Helping begins with permission**, not intervention.

That openness depends on trust. If someone declines, I step back and make sure they know that my offer remains open. I will continue to pay attention and may check in again later.

Offering support should help someone move forward without taking away their agency.
