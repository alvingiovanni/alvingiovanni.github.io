## Problem

The business lacked a clear view of how customers progressed through the **acquisition funnel**, from their first marketing interaction to a higher-value purchase. Conversion windows were undefined, and neither upsell nor cross-sell paths had been ranked. Observed conversions were also counted without determining whether an earlier interaction had caused them.

This made it difficult to time retargeting and nurture campaigns, compare conversion rates consistently, and determine which customer journeys represented genuine **incremental growth opportunities**.

## Solution

Led a program of **funnel diagnostic analyses** covering conversion timing, cross-product behavior, incrementality, pricing, and upsell economics.

Measured the time from first contact to purchase across four customer and product segments. These findings established conversion windows that could be applied consistently to campaign timing and performance measurement.

Mapped upsell and cross-sell journeys to identify the audiences most likely to progress toward higher-value purchases. Incrementality testing then separated **caused conversion from observed correlation**, providing a more reliable basis for attribution.

The broader analysis also modeled upsell economics and independently validated pricing experiments before their results were used for business decisions.

- Increased funnel efficiency by 22%
- Increased funnel revenue by 8%
