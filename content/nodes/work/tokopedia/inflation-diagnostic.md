## Problem

During a period of rising national inflation, lending disbursement growth slowed and fell short of target. Inflation was assumed to be the primary cause, but the aggregate trend did not show whether the slowdown affected the entire seller base or was concentrated in specific product categories.

The team needed to identify which seller segments remained resilient to inflation and where lending still had room to grow.

## Solution

Built a **platform-level inflation index** using the same methodology as the national index, then compared inflation patterns across product categories.

Analyzed seller transaction volume by category to identify where demand remained resilient despite higher prices and broader economic pressure. Recent category trends were then used to project growth and size the lending opportunity within **resilient, underserved segments**.

## Impact

- ~30% revenue uplift in the targeted verticals identified by the analysis
- Lending and growth efforts shifted toward seller segments that remained resilient to inflation
