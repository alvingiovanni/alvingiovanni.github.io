I’ve always been drawn to hobbies with a social and competitive side. I grew up playing sports, especially football, and enjoy how they bring people together through **teamwork and friendly competition**. Multiplayer games give me a similar outlet, challenging me to coordinate with teammates and make decisions under pressure.

For quieter downtime, I tend to follow my curiosity. I grew up watching Discovery Channel and still enjoy informative shows. I began reading fiction, with *The Alchemist* remaining my favourite book, and later became interested in books about **communication, leadership, and collaboration**.

Most of all, I value time with my wife and family. I’ve recently started joining my wife in the kitchen, usually as her sous chef, and we enjoy spending time with both sides of our family.
