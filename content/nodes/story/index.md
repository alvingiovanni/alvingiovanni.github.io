I didn't start in data. I trained as a civil engineer, then spent a few years as an assistant
project manager and later an operations analyst at RPX–FedEx — coordinating timelines,
tracking delivery, and getting increasingly interested in the reporting side of that work.

In 2021 I went through a data science bootcamp and moved into analytics full-time, landing at
Tokopedia (Bytedance) on their PayLater fintech team, then into growth strategy and analytics
leadership at Mindvalley.

The engineering background still shows up in how I work — I like structure, and I like things
that don't fall over under load.
