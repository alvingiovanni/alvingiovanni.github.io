Graduated with a **Bachelor’s degree in Civil Engineering** and began my career in engineering and operations roles. During my early carrers, I found myself increasingly drawn to the analytical side of the work, where I use data to understand performance, identify problems, and support better decisions.

That interest grew into a genuine passion for data. I began teaching myself data science and later completed a Data Science Bootcamp, which helped me transition into my first professional data role at **Tokopedia**. This opportunity exposes me to deeper collaboration with business and product leaders, becoming a key contributor to the business strategy and growth.

I later joined  **Mindvalley**, where my role expanded further into growth strategy and analytics. I now work closely with senior leadership (C-suites and execs) across marketing and product, helping shape investment decisions, measurement frameworks, LTV and CAC strategy, attribution, and marketing effectiveness.

Overall, my career has evolved from engineering into analytics, and from analytics into a broader role at the intersection of  **data, growth, product, and business strategy** . That journey has shaped how I approach problems today: with a strong analytical foundation, a practical understanding of business, and a willingness to navigate complexity and ambiguity to find effective solutions.
