I trained as a civil engineer and started out in engineering and operations roles: first as an **Assistant Project Manager at Tatamulia** (2017–2018), coordinating cross-functional teams on construction projects and building the reporting that tracked quality and delivery, then as an **Operations Analyst at RPX–FedEx** (2019–2020), working on operational efficiency and the expansion of a logistics network across Indonesia.

Neither was a data job on paper. In both, the part of the work I kept gravitating towards was the same: using data to understand performance, find the problem underneath the symptom, and give the people making decisions something firmer to stand on.

That pull is what turned into a career. I taught myself data science alongside the day job, completed a [Data Science Bootcamp](#/education) in 2021, and moved into my first professional data role at [Tokopedia](#/tokopedia) the same year. The engineering background never really left: checking the foundation before building on it is still how I approach an analysis.
