## Problem

Incomplete data, conflicting records, and inconsistent metric definitions made it difficult for stakeholders to trust the available reporting. Answering basic performance questions often required manual validation, making business decisions harder and slower.

The issue extended across data collection, revenue reconciliation, and business reporting. Without a consistent source of truth, stakeholders lacked a clear view of performance and the confidence to act quickly.

## Solution

Led the work to **rebuild trust in the data** by closing gaps across incomplete and conflicting datasets.

Closed gaps in lead and user data by bringing missing acquisition sources into the governed data model, defining how ambiguous sources should be classified, and verifying the resulting data through revenue attribution. Reconciled payment and revenue data against source systems and quantified leakage from payment failures and checkout disruptions.

Built **governed, standardized reporting** across marketing, business, and product metrics. This reporting covered acquisition, retention, revenue, and broader marketing performance, with each view validated against its source data to provide a consistent basis for decision-making.

## Impact

- Rebuilt stakeholders’ trust in core marketing and revenue data
- Gave stakeholders better visibility into lead sources, funnel performance, and revenue
- Quantified previously unmeasured revenue leakage across payment and checkout issues
- Created a trusted data foundation for subsequent attribution, marketing mix modeling, LTV, and lead-scoring initiatives
