---
title: Equal Respect
order: 4
---

No title, role, or position makes one person more deserving of dignity than another. **Everyone deserves the same basic respect**, and no one should be treated as though they are beneath someone else.

Equal respect does not mean treating everyone identically. People need different things, have different comfort zones, and experience the same actions differently. Respect means paying attention to those differences while treating each person’s needs as worthy of consideration.
