---
title: Effectiveness Before Efficiency
order: 2
---

**A solution must work before it can be optimized.** When there is a goal, my first priority is finding a real path toward achieving it. Once that path exists, I can look for ways to make it simpler, faster, or more efficient.

Effectiveness also begins with choosing a goal worth pursuing. When that choice is mine, I test it against the Golden Rule. The test is imperfect, but at minimum I do not want to pursue something I would hate to experience from the other side.
