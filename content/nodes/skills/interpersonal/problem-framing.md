title: Problem Framing
order: 6
---
Figuring out what question is actually worth answering before writing any SQL. This means interrogating the business request, identifying the real decision being made, and agreeing on what "right" looks like before the analysis starts — which saves significant rework downstream.

**See it in action**
- [Inflation & Market Diagnostic](#/inflation-diagnostic) — reframed a revenue decline question into a market vs. execution diagnostic
- [What Makes BNPL Succeed](#/bnpl-perspective) — reframed a product question into a competitive strategy question
