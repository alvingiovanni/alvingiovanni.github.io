## Problem

Customers often interacted with multiple marketing channels before converting. Traditional first-touch and last-touch attribution credited revenue to only one of those interactions, making it difficult to assess how much each channel contributed across the customer journey.

This created the risk that some channels were **systematically undervalued**, leading to marketing investment decisions based on an incomplete view of channel performance.

## Solution

Developed a **multi-touch attribution framework and system** that distributed revenue credit across the marketing touchpoints involved in each customer journey.

This provided a more objective view of each channel’s revenue contribution and identified several **underinvested channels**. The findings informed a shift in marketing strategy, directing more investment and effort toward channels whose contribution had previously been understated.
