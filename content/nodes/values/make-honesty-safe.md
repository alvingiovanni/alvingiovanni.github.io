---
title: Make Honesty Safe
order: 3
---

Honesty can be uncomfortable, and that is fine. The more important question is whether people feel **safe enough to say the difficult thing**, admit a mistake, or challenge an idea.

I dislike responses such as “I told you so” because they turn reflection into punishment. When someone acknowledges a mistake, I want to recognize the effort it took to reflect and then offer to work through the problem together.
