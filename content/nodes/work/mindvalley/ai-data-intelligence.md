## Problem

Business teams needed a faster way to explore performance data and answer recurring questions. Getting to an insight could take between one and two days, while many leadership questions required support from an analyst.

The initiative aimed to create a **self-service analytical layer** that could shorten this process while making business data easier to access and interpret.

## Solution

Developed internal application as a custom data visualization platform with **automatically generated AI insights** for its dashboards. These insights helped users interpret dashboard results and identify relevant findings without waiting for a separate manual analysis.

The platform also included an **AI agent chatbot** that users could query with business questions, providing a conversational way to explore the available data.

## Impact

- Reduced time to insight from between 1 and 2 days to under 1 hour
- Made roughly 80% of leadership’s recurring questions answerable without analyst support
