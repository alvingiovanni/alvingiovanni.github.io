## Problem

Marketing had a long list of channels — push, email, WhatsApp, social, and more — each with a
different cost, and no consistent way to know which one a given user actually responded to.
Users were often reached across several channels for the same message, which was both
expensive and a poor experience.

## Approach

Led development of a Media Acquisition / channel-affinity model using an RFE
(Recency–Frequency–Engagement) framework — a variant of classic RFM scoring, swapping
"monetary" for "engagement" — to rank each user's channel preference and route campaigns to
the single cheapest, highest-affinity channel they were likely to respond to.

## Impact

12% reduction in marketing spend and a 20% uplift in conversion, by prioritizing cheaper,
higher-affinity channels over blanket multi-channel outreach.
